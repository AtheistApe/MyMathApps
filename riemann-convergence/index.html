Riemann Sum Convergence

Every Riemann sum for a partition is trapped between the lower sum and the upper sum. Squeeze them together by increasing n.

For any choice of tags, mi ≤ f(xi*) ≤ Mi on each subinterval, so Ln ≤ Sn ≤ Un. Because f is continuous on the closed interval [a, b] it is uniformly continuous there, which forces Un − Ln → 0. The purple segment collapses to a single point, and every Riemann sum — no matter how the tags are chosen — is squeezed to that common value: the definite integral.

Syntax: x or t for the variable; ^ for powers; pi, e; sin cos tan sec csc cot asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh exp ln log sqrt cbrt abs. Implicit multiplication works: 2x, 3sin(x). The interval fields accept expressions too, e.g. 2pi.
